MSc student in Industrial Engineering (System Modeling & Data Analysis) at Iran University of Science and Technology (IUST). I work on **knowledge graphs, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG / GraphRAG) and AI for education**.

## Thesis

**Developing an AI-based interactive approach to increase the productivity of education: a case study of a machine-learning course**

<p dir="rtl" lang="fa">توسعه رویکرد تعاملی مبتنی بر هوش مصنوعی برای افزایش بهره‌وری آموزش: مطالعه موردی درس یادگیری ماشین</p>

The thesis builds a knowledge graph of a machine-learning course and a tutor that answers by walking that graph, then tests it with students against a plain chatbot and ordinary text retrieval.

**[Open the interactive thesis website →](/thesis/)** It has timelines of AI and education, the Iran education map, the ML-courses study, the evaluation datasets, who builds AI for education, and the research design. It is in English and Persian.
