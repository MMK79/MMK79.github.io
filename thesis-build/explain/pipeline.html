The pipeline, step by step

Follow one student question: data, chunks, index, knowledge graph, reranking, the graph walk and the final answer.

Read in this order: each page builds on the one before it.

01Interactive story

Our data pipeline

Eighteen stages from a scanned course, eight textbooks and two libraries to a passage store and a knowledge graph: routing by text layer, OCR, Markdown with page markers, table-of-contents units, bge-m3, the search store, the full graph branch and where each step runs. Every stage opens to its real numbers and says whether it is done, in the app, running or planned.

 02Interactive story

Chunking strategies

Seven ways to cut a document for retrieval, on six minutes of a real lecture: cut it yourself, see what the retriever returns, what the research says, and the rule our thesis uses.

 03Interactive story

Indexing for RAG

Exact search, BM25, learned sparse, IVF, HNSW, product and binary quantization, ColBERT, hybrid fusion, filters, rerankers and graph indexes, built for real on 1,076 lecture chunks: change the settings and see recall, work and memory move.

 04Interactive story

How a knowledge graph is built

How a typed knowledge graph is built, opened stage by stage on a real pilot over two CS 156 lectures (chunks, schema-guided extraction, entity resolution, an entailment gate, graph files, a typed question), next to the full build over the course and eight textbooks: 8,069 passages, 6,814 concepts and 5,866 eligible edges in the frozen graph.

 05Interactive story

Rerankers: a careful second look

A fast search finds 50 chunks; a cross-encoder reads each one with the question and reorders them. On our paraphrase question it lifted the answer from 33rd to 2nd, but only when the answer was in its list.

 06Interactive story

Graph hops and ranking: why the far hops vanished

The walk reached hops 1 and 2, but rule 7 gave all 10 slots to hop 0. Compare the balanced 4/3/3 quota, and how other systems choose depth. The frozen setting uses 30 graph slots: 10/10/10 when each hop has enough passages.

 07Interactive story

What happens when a student asks

One real CS 156 question, step by step through the five arms of the experiment on the pilot graph (linking, a typed walk, evidence, fusion, the 4,000-token budget), then one turn as built in the tutor app: gate, search, the answer model's name, "not in the material" anywhere in the reply, and the sentence check.
