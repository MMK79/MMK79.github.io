How to read this page

Evidenced: title, authors and first-version date of every arXiv entry (arXiv API); DOI entries resolved in OpenAlex or Crossref; the paper counts and their queries.

Ours or from memory: the one-line summaries, the impact lines, era names, significance and the affiliations. A tag unverified marks an event or a venue that was not checked against a primary source. Corrections are shown on the card of the event they changed.

Counts: phrase matches, so they miss papers that say only “RAG”. Read the shape, not the level. OpenAlex indexes abstracts late, so early years are low; arXiv is the better check.

Event text stays in English on the Persian page. The video is English only.

Data: vault note “History of RAG and GraphRAG”, files timeline.json, people.json, counts.json, views.json, citations.json (OpenAlex citation counts) and warnings.json (checked warning wording), read at build time. Video: the animations project, RagVsGraphRag.mp4.
