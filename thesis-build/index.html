Can a tutor that knows how a course fits together teach machine learning better than one that only searches its text?

An MSc thesis in Industrial Engineering (System Modeling & Data Analysis) at Iran University of Science and Technology. It builds a knowledge graph of a machine-learning course, a tutor that answers by walking that graph (GraphRAG), and tests it with students against a plain chatbot and ordinary text retrieval.
