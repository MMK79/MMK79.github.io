Can a tutor that knows how a course fits together teach machine learning better than one that only searches its text?

An MSc thesis in Industrial Engineering (System Modeling & Data Analysis) at Iran University of Science and Technology. It builds a knowledge graph of a machine-learning course and a tutor that answers by walking that graph (GraphRAG).

Method
Pre-registered: five arms and a locked question bank, scored by two separate judge models.
Now
Pilot 1 is done and the protocol was amended four times. Pilot 2 is next.
Result
No main-run result yet.
