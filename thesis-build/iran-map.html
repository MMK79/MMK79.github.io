Iran education map

Students, teachers and university staff in each of Iran's 31 provinces, by year, with girls and boys (women and men) counted apart. Click a province to see its numbers and its trend; click the sea to go back to all of Iran.

  • Boundary set by a treaty
  • No treaty: a median line or an unsettled line
  • Area claimed by two states
  • Iran's maritime zone

Dashed lines have no boundary treaty in the source: Marine Regions draws a median line there or marks the line as unsettled. This holds for Iran's lines with Azerbaijan and Turkmenistan in the Caspian Sea, with Iraq, Kuwait and Pakistan, and for parts of the lines with Oman, the United Arab Emirates and Saudi Arabia.

Sea boundaries: Flanders Marine Institute (VLIZ), Marine Regions, Maritime Boundaries Geodatabase, exclusive economic zones; CC BY 4.0; simplified for this map. doi.org/10.14284/632
