RAG evaluation metrics

Each metric answers one question about a RAG or GraphRAG system. The cards are grouped by layer, from the vector index up to reporting and reliability.

ProblemOne number ('accuracy') hides where a RAG system fails: retrieval, generation or the graph.
Why the next ideaEach layer fails in its own way, so each needs its own measure, and each measure has a blind spot.
FixOne card per metric: the question, the formula, a worked example you can change, and what the metric cannot see.

Open a card to load its video and its demo. In a demo, change the inputs and watch the result move. 'Real example' means the numbers come from an actual run on a small demo corpus (138 Wikipedia passages, 24 questions); it is not an evaluation of the thesis system. 'Toy example' means the inputs are invented for teaching.

The catalogue text, the videos and the demos are in English; only the page controls are translated.
