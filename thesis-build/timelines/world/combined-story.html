How to read this page

Scroll down through time. Each card says what it said (the idea, in one line) and what it caused (the measured impact, with its source and date).

• Click a tick on the strip at the top to jump to that event.
• Key milestones shows just the events on the one-page slide; Story (the default) adds the main measurements; All detail adds every detail-level figure (significance 3).
• Cards marked unverified have a figure we could not confirm from a primary source.
• ? or Esc toggles this panel.
